Dataset: Major Crops — Volume of Production, by Region, Quarter, and Semester, 2010–2026

Source:    Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) — OpenSTAT
URL:       https://openstat.psa.gov.ph/
Format:    CSV (wide format, ~858 KB)
License:   Philippine government open data (public domain)
Retrieved: September 2026

Description:
  This dataset contains quarterly production volume data (in metric tons)
  for 57 major Philippine crops across 17 administrative regions, covering
  the period from 2010 to 2026.  Each row represents a (Crop, Region) pair,
  and the columns contain production volumes for each Year × Quarter,
  Semester, and Annual aggregate.

Crops covered:
  Abaca, Banana (6 varieties), Cacao, Calamansi, Camote, Cassava, Coconut,
  Coffee (6 varieties), Eggplant, Mango (4 varieties), Mongo, Onion
  (4 varieties), Pineapple, Potato, Rubber, Sugarcane (5 categories),
  Tobacco (3 varieties), Tomato, Ampalaya, Cabbage.

Regions covered:
  CAR, Region I–XIII, MIMAROPA, NIR, BARMM.

Notes:
  - Values of ".." indicate missing or suppressed data.
  - The "PHILIPPINES" row is the national aggregate (sum of all regions).
  - Some crop sub-categories (e.g., Coffee Arabica) may have sparse data
    in certain regions.
